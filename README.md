## ShopNest Stores Sales - Power BI Capstone Project
# 📘 Project Overview
This Power BI capstone project provides an in-depth data analysis of ShopNest Stores, a leading department store on e-commerce marketplaces in Portugal. The project explores various aspects of the business through interactive dashboards and visualizations to uncover actionable insights that drive operational optimization and business growth.

# 🎯 Business Objectives
Identify top-performing product categories and regional markets

Analyze order fulfillment efficiency and delivery performance

Understand customer payment preferences and product satisfaction

Discover seasonal sales patterns and revenue trends

Provide data-driven recommendations for business strategy

# 📊 Key Analyses Performed
1. Top Categories by Revenue
Identified top 10 product categories by total sales

Health & Beauty leads with $1.26M in revenue

2. Delayed Orders Analysis
Analyzed delayed orders across product categories

Bed, Bath & Table category showed highest delays (811 orders)

3. Monthly Order Performance
Compared delayed vs. on-time orders monthly

August showed best performance with 11,620 early orders

4. Payment Method Analysis
Credit cards dominate (73.9% of transactions)

Boleto is second most popular (19.0%)

5. Product Rating Analysis
Identified top and bottom rated products

CDs, DVDs & Musicals highest rated (4.67/5)

6. State-wise Sales Distribution
São Paulo leads with $5.20M revenue (38.3% of total)

Regional performance analysis across Brazilian states

7. Seasonal Sales Patterns
Quarterly sales analysis showing growth trends

Q2 shows highest cumulative sales at $4.1M

8. Revenue Trend Analysis
Significant growth from 2016 ($47K) to 2018 ($8.45M)

Visualized year-over-year revenue progression
